Frequently Asked

What makes Lucknowi (Awadhi) biryani different from Hyderabadi biryani?

Awadhi biryani is cooked dum pukht style — par-cooked basmati rice and marinated meat are layered in a handi, sealed with dough and steamed slowly over a low flame. The result is gentler on spice and more aromatic than the hotter, tangier Hyderabadi style.

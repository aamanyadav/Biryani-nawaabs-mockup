Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Biryani Nawaabs ("we", "us") operates the website biryaninawaabs.com. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how we handle it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, phone number, email address and message.
  • Franchise application form: your name, mobile number, email address, city and state, investment budget range, preferred outlet format, and anything you add in the message field.

We collect this information only when you submit a form, and only with your consent (the franchise form includes an explicit consent checkbox).

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or order request.
  • To evaluate and respond to your franchise application.
  • To contact you about the enquiry you made. We do not add you to marketing lists without asking.

Who we share it with

Form submissions are delivered to us through our form-processing provider, which transmits your submission to our inbox. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and application details for as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterwards, after which they are deleted.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set tracking cookies of its own. The embedded Google Map in the Locations section is served by Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask us to access, correct or erase the personal information you have given us, or withdraw your consent. To do so, call us or write to the contact below.
